陀螺实验室隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 21 日

本政策说明陀螺实验室（下称"本游戏"）如何处理你的信息。本游戏由个人开发者 Haonian Zhou（下称"我们"）开发和发布。

1. 我们不收集个人信息

本游戏不需要注册或登录账号，不收集你的姓名、电话、邮箱、位置、通讯录、照片、设备标识符或其他个人信息，也不使用第三方统计、广告或追踪工具。

2. 游戏存档保存在你的设备上

你的游戏进度、零件、设置等数据只保存在你自己的设备本地，不会上传到我们的服务器。删除本游戏即会删除这些数据。

3. 网络使用

本游戏的核心玩法可以离线运行。如果今后加入需要联网的功能（例如公告、云存档、应用内购买或广告），我们会在加入前更新本政策，并说明涉及哪些数据、用于什么目的。

4. 应用商店提供的信息

如果你在设备上选择与开发者共享诊断数据，Apple（App Store / TestFlight）或其他应用商店可能会向我们提供匿名的崩溃报告和使用统计。这些信息由应用商店按其隐私政策收集和处理，我们只用于修复问题和改进游戏。

5. 儿童

本游戏不会有意收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁（或你所在地区规定年龄）以下的儿童。

6. 政策更新

我们可能会更新本政策。更新后的版本会发布在本页面，并修改上方的生效日期。

7. 联系我们

如对本政策有任何问题，请发邮件至：spinningtoplab@icloud.com

Spinning Top Lab Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy explains how Spinning Top Lab (the "Game") handles your information. The Game is developed and published by Haonian Zhou, an individual developer ("we", "us").

1. We do not collect personal information

The Game does not require an account or sign-in. It does not collect your name, phone number, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal information, and it does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

2. Your game data stays on your device

Your progress, parts, and settings are stored only locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. Deleting the Game deletes this data.

3. Network use

The Game's core gameplay works offline. If we add features that use the internet in the future (for example news, cloud saves, in-app purchases, or ads), we will update this policy before they are released and explain what data is involved and why.

4. Information provided by app stores

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers on your device, Apple (App Store / TestFlight) or other app stores may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These are collected and processed by the app store under its own privacy policy; we use them only to fix problems and improve the Game.

5. Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the age required in your region).

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please email: spinningtoplab@icloud.com
