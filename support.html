常见问题

我的存档保存在哪里？换手机或删除游戏会丢吗？

存档只保存在你的设备上。删除游戏会同时删除存档；目前还不支持云存档或换机迁移。

购买或退款问题怎么办？

应用内购买由 App Store / Google Play 处理。如需退款，请通过对应商店的退款渠道申请（App Store 可在 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请）。如果购买后物品没有到账，请发邮件给我们并附上购买记录截图。

游戏需要联网吗？

核心玩法可以离线游玩。

游戏卡顿或发热怎么办？

请先关闭其他后台应用并重启游戏。如果仍然卡顿，请把手机型号发给我们，方便我们优化。

Contact us

For questions, bug reports, or suggestions, email spinningtoplab@icloud.com. We usually reply within a few days.

When reporting a problem, please include your device model, OS version, game version, and a screenshot or screen recording if possible.

FAQ

Where is my progress saved? Will I lose it if I delete the game or change phones?

Your progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the game also deletes your progress; cloud saves and device transfer are not supported yet.

Purchases and refunds

In-app purchases are handled by the App Store / Google Play. To request a refund, please use your store's refund process (for the App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com). If a purchased item did not arrive, email us with a screenshot of your receipt.

Do I need an internet connection?

The core gameplay works offline.

The game lags or my phone gets hot

Close other background apps and restart the game. If it still lags, email us your device model so we can optimize for it.

Privacy

The game does not collect personal information. See our Privacy Policy.
